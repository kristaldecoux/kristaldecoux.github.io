Prepared for the Facet interview team

I’m a Facet member. I’d like to help more people find it.

I’ve led web strategy, conversion and experimentation at Ancestry, AWS and Elastic for more than 15 years. This page maps that experience to the Director of Web & Conversion Optimization role and shows how I’d approach the first few months.

Why Facet

I’ve been a Facet member since 2023, and I love the service. I know firsthand what it takes to trust a financial advisor you first met on a website, and that first impression is the experience this role is responsible for.

The role also matches the work I like best: a site in the middle of a transformation, a conversion program to build, and search changing faster than most teams can keep up with.

    At Elastic, I led the redesign of the homepage, platform page and three solutions pages, and we shipped all five a day ahead of a hard deadline. At AWS, I led the move of more than 800 product pages to a new set of templates.

How I’d start

  1. Listen first. Meet marketing, product, engineering, data science and compliance partners. Review the new homepage’s launch results, the funnel by traffic source and the current test backlog.
  2. Agree on the measurement baseline. Settle KPIs, funnel definitions and event standards with Data Science & Analytics, so every page in the rollout launches instrumented.
  3. Plan the rest of the rollout. A page-by-page plan with a shared structure, search requirements and compliance review built in, working back from the year-end deadline.
  4. Start testing where the traffic is. The first tests go on the highest-volume paid and organic landing pages, ranked by reach, impact, confidence and effort.
  5. Scope e-commerce. Map how members choose and buy today, and define what a self-serve purchase path needs before anything is built.

What I’d want to learn

  • What did the new homepage launch teach you about your visitors?
  • Which traffic sources bring the members who stay the longest?
  • Where does compliance review happen today, and how long does it take?
  • What does e-commerce mean for Facet: self-serve sign-up, new products, or both?
