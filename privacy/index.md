# Privacy

This is a personal portfolio site. It has no accounts, no forms and no ads, and it collects as little as possible.

## What this site collects

- **Visit counts through Vercel Web Analytics.** The site counts page views and records aggregated details such as the referring site, country, device type and browser. It doesn’t use cookies, and I can’t see who you are. I use it only to understand which pages are useful.
- **Server logs kept by the host.** Like any website, the hosting providers (Vercel, and GitHub Pages for the backup copy) may log technical request data such as IP addresses to run and secure their service.
- **Fonts from Google Fonts.** The site’s typefaces load from Google, which receives standard request information such as your IP address when your browser downloads them.

## What this site doesn’t do

- No cookies for tracking or advertising.
- No forms, sign-ups or newsletters, so no personal information is submitted here.
- No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
- Links to LinkedIn, cal.com and other sites follow those sites’ own privacy policies once you leave this one.
- The “Schedule a call” button opens my booking page on cal.com. Cal.com handles what you enter there, and I receive your name, email and answers with the booking.

If you email me, I use your message only to reply. Questions about this page can go to <kmdecoux@gmail.com>.
