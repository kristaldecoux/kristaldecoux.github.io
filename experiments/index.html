What the tests taught me

Six principles

Data-driven hypotheses

Combine quantitative and qualitative data, from analytics and heatmaps to session recordings, usability studies and focus groups, so ideas come from evidence, not preference.

Meet visitors where they are

Match the message to what each visitor already knows. Someone new needs to understand what you offer before news or promotions mean anything.

Not all friction is bad friction

Cut steps that add nothing. A step that adds context can mean fewer clicks but better-qualified signups and higher conversion.

Guide the choice

Personalized, guided paths win. Too much choice before a purchase can stall the decision.

The audience decides

“Contact sales” lost at Elastic and won at AWS. Test the path for each audience and industry.

Measure to the money

Trial starts, form fills, billthrough and lifetime value decide the result, not clicks.

Operating model

How I run a program

The same seven steps in every program I’ve built, with launch emails, a public Slack channel, a shared test calendar and leadership readouts so everyone sees the results.

Set goals and tracking

Tie each test to signups, leads or revenue, and confirm it’s measured before launch.

Scope sample and timing

Estimate visitors and duration up front, so data is neither under- nor oversampled.

Identify opportunities

Analytics, heatmaps, session recordings and user research.

Write the hypothesis

If we change X, we’ll see Y, because Z. Evidence, not taste.

Build and QA

In whichever platform fits, across devices and browsers, with no launches mid-test.

Analyze the impact

Significance on primary and secondary KPIs, then lifetime value to size the annual gain.

Ship, iterate, share

Winners become the default. Losers stop at the planned sample. Every result goes on the wiki.

AWS, then Elastic

Building the program, not just the tests

A testing program scales only when marketers bring the ideas. These are the habits and tools I built at AWS and brought to Elastic.

Deciding what to test

Many kinds of data surface opportunities, and each finding gets the right next step:

JUST DO IT
An obvious fix, no test needed
INSTRUMENT
A tracking or reporting gap to close first
HYPOTHESIZE
Not working, but the fix isn’t obvious yet
INVESTIGATE
Cause unknown, dig deeper before a hypothesis
TEST
A clear opportunity to test and optimize

RICE = Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort

Each is scored 1–5 on defined scales. Ideas are ranked into a roadmap.

A culture of experimentation

  • Brainstorm workshops. Testing basics, a data deep dive, writing a strong hypothesis, then an idea brainstorm. Partners learn testing, and the team leaves with a roadmap. Run at AWS (Homepage) and Elastic (Security journey mapping).
  • Monthly program review. Open to all marketers: running tests, last month’s results and what’s launching next. It opens with a “Which test won?” icebreaker vote, so people participate instead of multitasking.
  • Training partners. Intro to A/B Testing: sample size, significance and MDE, so stakeholders bring stronger ideas.
  • Templates and a wiki. The end-to-end process, scoping rules and outcomes, self-serve.
  • Keeping everyone informed. Launch and results emails, Slack updates, a shared test calendar and leadership reviews.
