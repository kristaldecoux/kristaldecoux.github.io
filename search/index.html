Found by people, and by the AI that answers them

More and more questions are answered by AI overviews and assistants before anyone clicks. My approach to search makes content easy for people, search engines and AI models to find, understand and cite.

Three kinds of search

SEO
Search engine optimization: ranking in traditional search results.
AEO
Answer engine optimization: being the source for direct answers, featured snippets and AI overviews.
GEO
Generative engine optimization: being accurately described and cited by AI assistants and large language models.

They overlap more than they differ. All three reward clear structure, accurate metadata and content that answers real questions.

My approach

Organize content around topics, not just pages

A topic-based structure helps people browse and helps AI models understand how ideas relate. Concept content should link clearly to the products that put those concepts to work.

Make every page machine-readable

Accurate titles and descriptions, structured data that describes what a page is, and copy written so a model can quote it correctly.

Build search into the content pipeline

Search review happens on the draft, not after launch. I built an SEO review tool so every content author gets recommendations, metadata and schema before publishing.

Keep the story consistent

AI assistants describe a company the way its content describes itself. Consistent positioning across pages leads to more accurate answers.

Start redesigns with search research

Competitive analysis and a content audit shaped the Elastic homepage redesign, identifying content gaps and keyword opportunities before a single wireframe.

Weigh search and conversion together

At Ancestry, a shorter headline on a high-ranking page lifted signups 5.6% and lifetime revenue per visitor 8.4%. We didn’t ship it, because the original headline held the keywords that brought the traffic. Instead we built a version of the page for paid traffic, where we could keep testing without risking search rank.

Measure visibility in AI answers too

Rankings and organic traffic still matter, but I also track how often our content appears in AI answers and overviews.

What we’re working on at Elastic

  • Information architecture for discoverability: a topic-based structure for concept content under /resources/learn/, with schema that cross-links concept and product pages.
  • An SEO review step in the content pipeline, run by authors on their own drafts.
  • Structured content and metadata across elastic.co to improve machine readability and crawlability.
  • Narrative positioning so AI answers describe Elastic accurately.

Since the content pipeline changed, Elastic’s content has been appearing more often in AI answers and overviews.
